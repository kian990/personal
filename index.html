about Native New Yorker. Born and bred. I'm a Creative Producer working at the intersection of culture, technology, and media. At 23, I've spent the last few years building a body of work spanning content strategy, video production, and creative direction, amassing tens of millions of views across platforms and leading brand presence through major product pivots at Zora, one of the most talked-about companies in web3.

My background in Computer Science gives me a different lens than most. I think in systems, move fast, and build things that actually work. That foundation, combined with hands-on experience across marketing, editing, social strategy, and creative production, means I can operate at every layer of a project from concept to execution.

Before Zora, I was embedded in the music industry, working across record labels, talent management, and artist projects. That's where I learned how culture actually moves before it hits the feed, and it informs everything I make now.

I'm drawn to work that sits at the edge of what's relevant and what's next. Brands and projects that take culture seriously and want to do something real with it. If you're building something at that intersection, I want to be in the room.
